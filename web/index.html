How it decides

It looks for the patterns behind common Indian scams: fake KYC and bank alerts, UPI collect requests, digital-arrest threats, parcel and electricity-bill messages, paid-task jobs and more. It also takes apart every link without opening it.

It cannot prove a message is safe. If a message asks for money, an OTP or a PIN, stop and call the company on the number printed on its official website or on the back of your card.
